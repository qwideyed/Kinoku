About Kinoku

Kinoku is an open, community-curated web index built to help people find working websites for Movies, TV Shows, Anime, Hentai, Torrents, and Forums in one organized place.

Instead of relying on outdated bookmarks or spammy search results, Kinoku organizes direct links by category and rates each site's ad behavior so you know what to expect before you click.

Why Kinoku Exists

Sites go down, change domains, or get flooded with intrusive ads. Kinoku keeps an updated, easy-to-browse list of active sites.

Community Driven

Anyone can suggest new websites or report broken links and annoying pop-ups through the Submit Site page or Discord.

No Accounts or Bloat

No sign-ups, no paywalls, and no tracking. Just open a category, pick a site, and browse safely with an ad-blocker.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does Kinoku host or stream any movies, anime, or videos?
No. Kinoku is strictly a web directory and link index. We do not host, upload, store, or stream any video files or media on our servers. All links lead to external third-party websites.
Is Kinoku free to use?
Yes, 100% free. You do not need to create an account or pay anything to browse the index. Donations are completely optional and only go toward keeping the site maintained.
Why do I need an ad-blocker or specific browser?
Many free third-party streaming and torrent sites rely on aggressive pop-up ads or redirects. Using an ad-blocker like uBlock Origin or a privacy browser like Brave, Firefox, DuckDuckGo, or BrowseHere blocks those ads and keeps your browsing safe.
How do I submit a website or report a dead link?
Click the Submit Site button in the top right corner to add a website to a category, or join our Discord server to report broken links, domain changes, or ad-score updates.
What are the different categories on Kinoku?
Movies/TV: Streaming and tracking sites for films and television series.
Anime: Streaming, database, and seasonal anime sites.
Hentai: Adult anime streaming and archive sites.
Index: Other directories, search engines, and aggregators.
Torrets: Public and semi-private torrent indexes.
Forums: Community discussion boards and release threads.

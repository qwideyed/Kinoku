Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Kinoku is an independent, read-only web index maintained by Qwideyed. We built Kinoku around strict data minimization and user anonymity—browsing the directory never requires an account, personal details, or invasive tracking. This Privacy Policy explains how the site works, what happens when you submit a site suggestion or report a link, and how external websites operate once you leave Kinoku.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Kinoku does not operate a user registration system, login portal, or user profile database. We do not ask for, collect, harvest, rent, or sell personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically, Kinoku never collects:

  • Real names, account passwords, or personal login credentials
  • Email addresses, phone numbers, or physical mailing addresses
  • Watch history, search queries, clickstream logs, or outbound click telemetry
  • Device hardware identifiers, GPS location data, or contact lists

3. How the Directory Is Maintained

All website listings, categories, ad-score ratings, and archived entries on Kinoku are curated and updated directly by the site developer (Qwideyed). Visitors cannot directly modify, edit, or delete entries on the live site; all directory updates are published directly through reviewed site builds.

4. Site Suggestions & Link Reports

Visitors may voluntarily submit a website suggestion for review or report a broken link, domain change, or intrusive pop-up ad using the Submit Site page or official community channels.

  • Only submit public website URLs, category details, and ad behavior notes.
  • Do not include your real name, email address, IP address, or any sensitive personal information in suggestion or report fields.
  • Submitted URLs are reviewed manually before being added, updated, or archived in the public directory.

5. Cookies, Trackers & Browser Fingerprinting

Kinoku does not set first-party tracking cookies, third-party advertising cookies, social media pixels, or canvas/audio fingerprinting scripts. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or behavioral ad networks anywhere on the site.

6. Static Hosting & Standard Network Logs

Kinoku is served as static web files. When your browser requests a page from our hosting provider or content delivery network (CDN), standard network metadata—such as your IP address, browser user-agent string, requested file path, and timestamp—is automatically transmitted over HTTPS as part of normal internet routing.

Our static infrastructure provider processes this technical metadata solely to serve pages reliably, enforce TLS encryption, and defend against automated denial-of-service attacks. Kinoku does not link this metadata to any individual identity.

7. External Third-Party Websites & Outbound Links

Kinoku is strictly a web index. Every website listed under Movies/TV, Anime, Hentai, Index, Torrets, and Forums is an independent third-party platform owned and operated outside of Kinoku's control.

Once you click an outbound link and leave Kinoku, this Privacy Policy no longer applies. External sites may deploy their own cookies, analytics, pop-up ads, redirect scripts, or third-party ad networks. Kinoku assumes no responsibility for the privacy practices, security, or content of any linked external website.

8. Recommended Privacy & Ad-Blocking Tools

Because third-party streaming, torrent, and forum websites frequently run intrusive advertising or tracking scripts, we strongly advise every visitor to use content-blocking software before opening external links:

  • Desktop Browsers: Install uBlock Origin (best paired with Firefox) to block pop-ups, malicious redirects, and third-party trackers.
  • Mobile Devices: Use Brave Browser or DuckDuckGo Browser for built-in tracker and pop-up protection.
  • Smart TVs: Use BrowseHere on Google TV and Android TV devices.
  • Additional Hardening: Avoid entering real email addresses, reusing passwords, or downloading executable files from unverified third-party streaming pages.

9. Donations & External Payment Processors

If you choose to support Kinoku via the Donate button, you will be redirected to an external payment platform (such as Ko-fi or PayPal). Kinoku never sees, processes, or stores your credit card number, bank details, or billing address. All payment transactions are governed exclusively by the privacy policy and security standards of the chosen payment provider.

10. Discord Community

If you join our Discord server to discuss sites, suggest additions, or report dead links, your participation is governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Please avoid sharing private or personally identifying information in public server channels.

11. Age Restrictions & Adult Categories

Kinoku is intended for general audiences, and certain sections of the index (such as the Hentai category) link to adult-oriented external websites intended strictly for individuals who are at least 18 years of age (or the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction). Kinoku does not knowingly collect any data from minors.

12. Updates to This Privacy Policy

Qwideyed reserves the right to revise or expand this Privacy Policy whenever new features, categories, or hosting configurations are introduced to Kinoku. Any updates take effect immediately upon being published on this page, reflected by the Effective Date at the top.

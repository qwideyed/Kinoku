About Kinoku

Kinoku is a curated web index of websites for watching and tracking Anime, Movies, and TV Shows. When visiting external websites listed below, always use an ad-blocker or a privacy browser with built-in ad-blocking:

uBlock Origin (Ad-Blocker) Brave Browser Firefox DuckDuckGo Browser

Minimum requirements for best free streaming:

  • 100% Free
  • 1080p or 720p
  • NO account required
  • NO geoblocking
  • Mobile friendly
  • Works with ad-blocker
  • English website
  • Easy to start watching
  • Easy to find S02E03
  • New movies/tv shows
  • Old movies/tv shows
  • Not hosted on free subdomain
  • I would personally use it daily

Ad-score explained:

  • Pop ads are not detected.
  • Pop ads are mostly blocked. May have captcha. Ad-blocker may interfere.
  • Pop ads may redirect from current page. Ad-blocker may interfere. Intrusive ads.
  • Test is done with Adblock Plus and uBlock Origin on desktop, and DuckDuckGo browser on mobile.
  • For more comprehensive ad-blocking, use Brave browser on mobile. Also recommended: BrowseHere for Google/Android TV.
  • How ads are implemented may change over time so always use an ad-blocker (or Firefox + uBlock Origin) when visiting these websites.

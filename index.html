About Kinoku

Kinoku is a curated web index of websites for watching and tracking Movies, TV Shows, and Anime. When visiting external websites listed on Kinoku, always use an ad-blocker or a privacy browser with built-in ad-blocking:

uBlock Origin (Ad-Blocker) Brave Browser Firefox DuckDuckGo Browser BrowseHere (Android/Google TV)

Minimum requirements for best free streaming:

  • 100% Free
  • 1080p or 720p
  • NO account required
  • NO geoblocking
  • Mobile friendly
  • Works with ad-blocker
  • English website
  • Easy to start watching
  • Easy to find S02E03
  • New movies/tv shows
  • Old movies/tv shows
  • Not hosted on free subdomain
  • I would personally use it daily

Ad-score explained:

  • Pop ads are not detected.
  • Pop ads are mostly blocked. May have captcha. Ad-blocker may interfere.
  • Pop ads may redirect from current page. Ad-blocker may interfere. Intrusive ads.
  • Test is done with Adblock Plus and uBlock Origin on desktop, and DuckDuckGo browser on mobile.
  • For more comprehensive ad-blocking, use Brave browser on mobile. I also recommend BrowseHere for Google/Android TV.
  • How ads are implemented may change over time so always use an ad-blocker (or Firefox + uBlock Origin) when visiting these websites.

Privacy Policy

How Kinoku handles user data and third-party websites.

Zero Data Collection

Kinoku does not require user registration, accounts, or personal information. Any sites you add locally are saved strictly in your own browser storage and are never transmitted to any server.

External Links & Third-Party Sites

Kinoku links to external third-party websites that have their own independent privacy policies, tracking scripts, and advertisements. We strongly recommend using an ad-blocker or privacy browser when visiting any external link.

Submission Guidelines

What Gets Approved

  • 100% free access without mandatory sign-ups
  • Clean 720p/1080p playback or reliable index
  • Works properly with uBlock Origin or Brave
  • Hosted on a proper domain (no free subdomains)

What Gets Rejected

  • Sites requiring paid subscriptions or forced accounts
  • Malware, fake download buttons, or scam redirects
  • Clone sites with broken players or dead catalogs

Privacy Reminder

Do not include personal details, emails, or account credentials in your submission. You can also drop suggestions directly in our Discord server.
